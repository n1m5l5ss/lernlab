## Keeping the credit

- **Required by the licence:** the MIT licence asks every copy of LernLab, and every substantial part of it, to keep the copyright line in [LICENSE](LICENSE), which names kandru as the creator. Removing it breaks the licence.
- **Required to use the name:** a fork or continuation may call itself LernLab only if it keeps the line "Originally created by kandru (aka n1m5l5ss)" in its README and on the app's About screen. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md#if-the-team-goes-quiet).
